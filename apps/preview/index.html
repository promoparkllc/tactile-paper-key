01Where it comes from

Object first. Interface second.

We didn’t start in a design tool. We built a desk keypad in Blender, pressed it, lit it, and then flattened it piece by piece until it became the player.

  1. 1 · BRIEF
    A desk objectKeys that latchA small displayOne glaze
    Hi-fi panels and desk instruments, not screens. Matte, quiet, one accent.
  2. 2 · THREE DIRECTIONS
    Graphite read as a default dark UI; Instrument got busy. Paper Key won.
  3. 3 · THE OBJECT Real parts, real numbers: Ø 52 keys, 2 mm travel, a 14×32 fader cap.
  4. 4 · THE FLATTEN The engraved ring became a 1.25 px border. The groove became the timeline.
  5. 5 · THE SKIN
    What ships: the same object, now CSS, over any footage.

▶ The film in the player above is this process, 26 seconds, rendered in Blender.

  1. 1 · PLAY KEYThe only orange key. Its glyph morphs between play and pause.
  2. 2 · DISPLAYState, speed and time in one black pill: PLAY 1.0×, PAUSED, LOADING, END.
  3. 3 · SEEK FADERA ribbed cap on an engraved line. Hover for a framed preview of that moment.
  4. 4 · MUTESpeaker glyph shows the level in waves; muted shows a cross.
  5. 5 · VOLUME FADERA short bone fader. Arrow keys move it in 5 % steps.
  6. 6 · CAPTIONSLatches down 1 px and takes a 2 px accent rim when on.
  7. 7 · SETTINGSSpeed, captions and quality, in a bone panel that opens from the key.
  8. 8 · FULL SCREENCorners out to enter, corners in to leave. The dock scales up to 1.25×.

05Accessibility

Readable over any frame.

The dock is opaque, so contrast never depends on the video. Switch the footage and watch nothing change.

  • 0axe (WCAG 2.1 AA) violations, desktop and phone, menus open. report
  • 13:1Ink on keys. Display text 16:1. Fills and fader lines ≥ 3:1 on every glaze.
  • 44 pxMinimum target everywhere, including the 2 px timeline.
  • 2 pxFocus ring, 3 px gap, separate from the pressed state. Keyboard only.
  • HCForced colours: keys, accent rims and focus map to system colours.
  • RMReduced motion: every state still changes; nothing travels.

Three lines. Apache 2.0.
