01Where it comes from

Object first. Interface second.

We didn’t start in a design tool. We built a desk keypad in Blender, pressed it, lit it, and then flattened it piece by piece until it became the player.

  1. 1 · BRIEF
    A desk objectKeys that latchA small displayOne glaze
    Hi-fi panels and desk instruments, not screens. Matte, quiet, one accent.
  2. 2 · THREE DIRECTIONS
    Graphite read as a default dark UI; Instrument got busy. Paper Key won.
  3. 3 · THE OBJECT Real parts, real numbers: Ø 44 keys, 2 mm travel, a 12×26 fader cap.
  4. 4 · THE FLATTEN The engraved ring became a 1.25 px border. The groove became the timeline.
  5. 5 · THE SKIN What ships: the same object, now CSS, over any footage.

▶ The film in the player above is this process, 27.5 seconds, rendered in Blender.

  1. The only orange key. Its glyph morphs between play and pause.
  2. State, speed and time in one black pill: PLAY 1.0×, PAUSED, LOADING, END.
  3. A ribbed cap on an engraved line. Hover for a framed preview of that moment.
  4. Speaker glyph shows the level in waves; muted shows a cross.
  5. A short bone fader. Arrow keys move it in 5 % steps.
  6. Latches down 1 px and takes a 2 px accent rim when on.
  7. Speed, captions and quality, in a bone panel that opens from the key.
  8. Corners out to enter, corners in to leave. The dock scales up to 1.25×.

05Accessibility

Readable over any frame.

The dock is opaque, so contrast never depends on the video. Switch the footage and watch nothing change.

  • 0axe (WCAG 2.1 AA) violations, desktop and phone, menus open. report
  • 13:1Ink on keys. Display text 16:1. Fills and fader lines ≥ 3:1 on every glaze.
  • 44 pxMinimum target everywhere, including the 2 px timeline.
  • 2 pxFocus ring, 3 px gap, separate from the pressed state. Keyboard only.
  • HCForced colours: keys, accent rims and focus map to system colours.
  • RMReduced motion: every state still changes; nothing travels.

07Install

Four steps. Any site.

  1. 01TERMINAL $ npm i @videojs/html@10
  2. 02TERMINAL $ npx degit promoparkllc/tactile-paper-key/src tactile-skin
  3. 03JS import '@videojs/html/video/player'; import './tactile-skin/video/skin';
  4. 04HTML 
    <video-player>
  <media-container class="media-skin media-container media-video"
                   data-theme="tactile" data-preset="video">
    <video src="clip.mp4" playsinline></video>
    <!-- then the rest of tactile-skin/video/skin.html, from <media-poster> on -->
  </media-container>
</video-player>
