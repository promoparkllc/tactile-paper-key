Mobile rules
stepsStack: label row (number, tag, copy key) above the code. Long commands wrap at “/”; HTML scrolls sideways inside its card, never the page.
copy keyVisual 32, hit area 44 × 44 (pseudo-element). Always visible on touch (no hover-only controls).
tokensFirst 4 rows visible, “+ 6 more” expands in place (button, aria-expanded). Tap copies; there's no hover link on touch, the parts highlight on tap.
drawingParts on a 3-column grid; the dock outline is hidden below 600 px (too small to read), the caption text keeps its numbers.
layout bugOn the live page the 06 title and drawing panel start past the left edge: align them to the same .wrap as the other sections.
